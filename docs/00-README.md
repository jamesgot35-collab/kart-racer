# Sparkdrift GP: Executive Summary

1. **What:** an original, premium-feel arcade kart racer for phones, in the spirit of a top console kart racer but with its own world, characters and music.
2. **Scale:** 100 original racers in 10 themed sets of 10, 42 kart bodies, 18 wheel styles in 5 sizes, 12 spoilers, 10 exhausts, 8 bumpers, 32 paints × 5 finishes, and 20 tracks (4 cups of 4 plus 4 bonus).
3. **Feel:** drift for a 3-tier boost, a timed start boost, drafting, bump rules by weight class, light rubber-banding, and 8 skill-based items that each have a counter.
4. **Modes:** Grand Prix (12-racer field), Time Trial with ghosts, Versus with 6-character code rooms for 2 to 4 humans (CPU fill to 12), optional Battle, Daily Challenge with a leaderboard.
5. **Economy:** coins from racing buy everything; money buys cosmetics only; every kart body shares one stat budget (no pay-to-win).
6. **Multiplayer:** unambiguous 20-symbol code alphabet, host-authoritative items with lag compensation, client prediction, host migration, CPU takeover on drop.
7. **Sound first:** class-specific layered engines, tiered drift/boost sounds, an announcer, character barks, per-track ambience, and a six-stem dynamic score that reacts to position and the final lap, through a ducking mixer and master limiter.
8. **Vertical slice:** 8 characters, 6 karts, full garage, 4 tracks, 4-player code lobbies, all items, 3-tier drift boost. A playable browser build ships alongside these docs.
9. **Production:** 24 months, ~85 people, $10M; Unity 6 + Nakama + FMOD recommended; risks and the performance budget are in `06`.
10. **Docs:** `01` game design, `02` roster, `03` karts/mods, `04` tracks, `05` vertical slice, `06` production/risks/perf. All names and stats are generated from one data source and verified by script.
